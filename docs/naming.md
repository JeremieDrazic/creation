# Creation — naming rationale

Status: Creation selected by the owner on 2026-10-06.

The name honors the Creator and connects the work to wonder at living nature. Use `creation` as the project name. No domain, trademark, or broader uniqueness checks have been performed.

## Confirmed criteria

- Poetic rather than a technical project label.
- Evokes living nature.
- Works in French and English.
- Broad enough to encompass light, water, leaves, birds, and future experiences.

The owner's initial idea is `nature-of-code`, but The Nature of Code is an existing book and the project should develop its own public identity. The owner has now requested renaming the local folder to `creation`.

## Earlier candidates — superseded

| Candidate | Creative association | Tradeoff to explore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anima | Breath, animating presence, life | Broad and evocative; distinctiveness is unverified. |
| Sylume | Invented blend suggesting woodland and light | More specific sonic identity; may imply forests more than water. Pronunciation must be tested. |
| Luma | Short, soft, luminous sound | Clear connection to light; living nature is less explicit. Distinctiveness is unverified. |
| Lumora | Invented luminous sound with a gentle cadence | Broad enough for a collection, but the nature association relies on the visual identity. |

Mentor's exploratory preference: Anima for living presence; Sylume for a more invented poetic identity. Neither was selected; both are superseded by Creation.

## Remaining naming work

Typography, logo, public domain, and experience names remain open.
