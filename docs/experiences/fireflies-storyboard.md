# Fireflies — visitor journey

The overall journey and musical-start label are confirmed. Detailed staging and timings remain to review with real motion/audio. This is not a track-authored choreography.

## Journey

| Phase | Visitor and living world | Interface and handover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Encounter | Observe, focus and type. Small lights orbit the input line with independent phases. | Labeled editable word, explicit submit, shared language/sound/navigation controls. Exact initial copy/audio consent presentation is open. |
| Formation | Lights approach letter targets and replace typography, then drift naturally. | Preserve silhouette/scale; withdraw input controls gracefully. Strong perturbation may wait for one clear first formation; exact handling is proposed. |
| Reverie | Gently perturb a readable living word; let it reform. Dreamy ambience does not drive particles. | Laisser danser / Let them dance, word editing, sound and exit. Reverie can continue indefinitely. |
| Musical handover | Ambient audio yields to the track; musical influence grows without resetting positions. | Shift to music controls; blend toward gentler local influence. |
| Musical passage | Frequency/amplitude/beat shape differentiated generative movement; word readability may disappear. | Keep sound, return and exit discoverable. No fixed spiral/wave choreography or automatic camera response to every beat. |
| Return | Musical influence subsides, ambience returns, word reforms from current positions. | Restore reverie actions. Same gentle return at track end or voluntary early return. |
| Continuation | Stay, edit, choose another musical passage or leave. | No forced completion screen, replay or next step. |

The atmosphere follows study 09, input composition study 11 and same-word states study 13 in the [moodboard](../../moodboard/fireflies/README.md). Still images do not validate movement or performance.

## Editing ritual — confirmed

Available during reverie; during music, first return to reverie.

1. Gradually remove gesture perturbation and recover the living word's shape.
2. Transform it into editable typography at the same position/perceived size; reveal underline and caret.
3. Keep autonomous background motion, input-axis lights and ambience alive without gesture influence.
4. Type in conventional text only; no particle-target reconstruction per keystroke.
5. Submit to transform the new word; cancel to restore the original.
6. Withdraw the input and progressively restore scene interaction.

A short typography/particle overlap may support one silhouette; never display separate editable and living words. Prefer continuous glyph shape, with a same-location crossfade fallback if more convincing or efficient.
