# Fireflies

Fireflies is the founding experience, revisiting the owner's Gobelins project. Its name is the working experience title. It has its own URL and is separate from Creation's homepage.

## Confirmed atmosphere

An abstract summer night: deep enveloping blue, luminous organic life, calm and depth. Follow [atmosphere study 09](../../moodboard/fireflies/studies/09-refined-luminous-hierarchy.md), [input study 11](../../moodboard/fireflies/studies/11-input-axis-fireflies-fr.md) and [state study 13](../../moodboard/fireflies/studies/13-single-word-states-fr.md).

Mix warm ivory, pale gold and subtle green-gold within depth layers. Keep distant/intermediate lights subdued, individual word particles distinct, and several larger dim proximity-blurred foreground lights. A discreet blue veil supports depth without an obvious nebula. Preserve dark space around text/UI. Exact color, density, brightness and focus values require motion/render review.

The supplied [particle reference](../../moodboard/fireflies/references/01-luminous-particles.jpg) informs materials/depth only; its brightness, spiral, branding and exact colors are not selected.

## Word and interaction

The visitor enters one word in conventional typography above a fine rule surrounded by small lights orbiting its horizontal axis. On submission, lights approach and replace the letter silhouette with living particles. Formation must feel continuous; refine it during implementation rather than require a separate preliminary prototype.

The editable and living word share position and perceived scale. Prefer a continuous silhouette; a same-position/same-size crossfade is an accepted fallback when it better preserves quality, fluidity or performance. Exact point correspondence is not mandatory.

Interactive reverie begins once the word is formed. Dreamy, nostalgic, soft ambient audio accompanies autonomous motion; fireflies do not react to this audio. Gesture influence may temporarily dissolve readability; the word reforms gradually when influence fades. Observation without input is a complete experience. Exact attraction/dispersal mappings, strengths and keyboard alternatives remain to test.

Editing is available in reverie in the current scene. Suspend gesture perturbation, reform the word, transform it into editable typography, reveal underline/caret and keep autonomous motion/ambience alive. Do not show two separate words or rebuild targets per keypress. Submit forms the new living word; cancel restores the original. Restore scene interaction progressively. During music, return to reverie before editing.

## Music-reactive passage

Offer a voluntary **Laisser danser / Let them dance** action in reverie; never auto-start after a timeout. Ambient sound gives way to the track while musical influence rises from current positions/values.

Movement emerges from the current music, without a predefined track choreography. Frequency, amplitude and beat each produce perceptible effects with different sensitivities across individuals. Changing the track must not require reauthoring movement. Visitor-supplied music is a future possibility, not a V1 upload/backend requirement.

Accepted starting mappings, to refine through listening and motion:

- Low frequencies: broad slow currents; mids: local weaving; highs: selective light accents.
- Amplitude: excursion and group expansion/contraction, with restrained light modulation.
- Beat: selective impulses/direction changes with variation, not scene-wide simultaneous flashing.

The word can become entirely unreadable during music. Gentle local pointer influence bends nearby trajectories while music retains global direction. Use stronger freedom in reverie and subtler influence in music; influence decays progressively. Music response must preserve the night and avoid constant bright flashes.

Track end or **Revenir à la rêverie** triggers a continuous return: musical response subsides, ambient sound/autonomous behavior return and the word reforms. No results screen or forced next action. Timing, abrupt endings, silence in a track and English return copy remain to refine.

## Sound and interruption

Muting music silences output promptly and gently returns to silent reverie; unmuting restores ambience without restarting the musical passage. Muting reverie only silences it. Communicate this behavior accessibly. Music restart semantics remain open.

Hidden-page suspension and rendering recovery preserve the passage rather than end it. See [runtime contracts](../runtime-contracts.md). Sound activation remains voluntary.

## Remaining local decisions

Word length/scripts/accents/empty-input handling; precise gestures and reduced-motion treatment; control placement/copy; music/ambient assets and restart behavior; numerical rendering/audio-analysis settings. Review these in context during bounded implementation. Interactive mobile is outside V1; 2D/3D mode selection remains a future possibility.
