# Creation — introductory experience

## Confirmed direction

Creation's homepage is an interactive experience, distinct from Fireflies. [Celestial-veil study 07](../../moodboard/intro/07-celestial-veil.md) is the specific visual target: darkness, deep ink-blue, layered fine stellar dust, depth and illumination under gesture. It contains no fireflies, particle word, bright nucleus, realistic landscape or earthly mist.

Preserve the image's composition, colors, density and illuminated-state balance. The veil arrives very discreetly and beautifully; text also enters with animation. Refine the implementation for motion, legibility and performance without silently substituting a different art direction. Exact reproduction and measured 60 fps are not established by the image.

Display the **complete Genesis 1:3**, using La Bible du Semeur in French:

> Et Dieu dit alors : Que la lumière soit ! Et la lumière fut.

Source: [Genesis 1:3, BDS](https://www.bible.com/fr/bible/21/GEN.1.3.BDS). The image's short excerpt is not the final copy. Line breaks may compose the text without changing its wording. English edition, attribution and publication permissions remain to confirm.

Include subtle ambient sound to be chosen later. Activation is voluntary, retained during the visit. V1 is desktop; mobile receives a designed desktop invitation.

## Staging proposals

Reveal dust through restrained opacity/depth, without an explosion or conspicuous zoom. Introduce the verse, reference and UI with small offsets and carefully phased motion; avoid letter-by-letter typing. The text should be readable without a required gesture.

Gesture influence illuminates nearby dust and fades gently. Preserve a rewarding contemplative scene without input. Keep Fireflies accessible without a mandatory intro duration. The navigation transition may withdraw the veil while the nocturnal scene/input arrives; no literal star-to-insect morph is required.

## Remaining decisions

Exact entrance/release timing, pointer field, keyboard/reduced-motion behavior, initial controls, full-verse layout, English scripture, sound assets and intro/Fireflies handover. [Runtime readiness](../runtime-contracts.md) and [shared interface](../interface-direction.md) define infrastructure without prescribing artistic choreography.
